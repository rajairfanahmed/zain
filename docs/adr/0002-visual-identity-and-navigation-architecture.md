# Kinetic Monolith Visual Identity & Tri-Zone Navigation Architecture

We decided to build Muhammad Zain-Ul-Abideen's platform with a Kinetic Monolith & Obsidian Specular aesthetic (`#07080B` canvas, hairline rules, high-contrast typography, and Lenis/GSAP motion choreography) paired with a structured Tri-Zone Navigation Bar (brand logo on the far left, canonical route links centered, and the Resume action button on the far right). This delivers an uncompromising Awwwards-tier visual impact while providing immediate, unhindered navigation for engineering hiring managers.
